Brings clarity to complexity.

You naturally make complicated ideas easier for others to understand. People come away from time with you knowing what matters and what to do next.

What this means

Illuminators don't just understand things; they need other people to understand them too. You notice the gap between what someone knows and what they need to know, and you feel pulled to close it.

This is less about being a good communicator and more about how you think. You understand something fully only once you can explain it simply. Explaining is how you learn, and teaching is how you find out what you believe.
