# Zyrro Detection Engine Spec v1

## Core Detection Principle

Zyrro does **not** detect what the user says they are.

It detects what their story repeatedly reveals.

### Priority Hierarchy

1. Repeated behaviour  
2. Repeated choices  
3. Repeated tensions  
4. Repeated energy  
5. Repeated outcomes

Self-description has the lowest evidentiary weight.

**Example**

Low-weight:  
“I think I’m strategic.”

High-weight:  
“I repeatedly built systems in unstable environments.”

**Behaviour > identity claim**

# Four Signal Types

These are the universal signal classes.

Every signature must be detected through one or more.

---

# Signal Type A — Energy Peaks

## Definition

What energises the user.

## Linguistic Indicators

- loved  
- alive  
- energised  
- excited  
- proud  
- absorbed  
- could do it for hours

**Reliability:** High  
**Effect:** Strong intensity multiplier

---

# Signal Type B — Frustration Patterns

## Definition

What repeatedly frustrates them.

Often inverse evidence.

## Examples

Builder frustration:  
“Nothing ever gets finished.”

Truth Seeker frustration:  
“People avoid saying what’s real.”

**Reliability:** High

---

# Signal Type C — Recurring Themes

## Definition

Repeated behavioural patterns across time.

Highest reliability.

## Examples

- repeated building  
- repeated mentoring  
- repeated restructuring  
- repeated initiating

Strong frequency signal.

---

# Signal Type D — Pride Stories

## Definition

What they volunteer without being asked.

Strong identity ownership.

## Indicators

- favourite achievements  
- defining wins  
- peak moments

Strong intensity signal.

---

# Scoring Model

Each signature receives:

- Frequency Score (F)  
- Intensity Score (I)

## Formula

**Final Score = F × I**

Range:

1–25

---

# Frequency Scoring Rules

Measures recurrence.

## 1
Appears once.
Weak evidence.

## 2
Appears twice.
Low confidence.

## 3
Appears across multiple chapters.
Clear pattern.

## 4
Appears consistently across life/career.
Strong pattern.

## 5
Dominant recurring pattern.
Core operating system.

---

# Intensity Scoring Rules

Measures energetic force.

## 1
Flat mention.
No emotional charge.

## 2
Mild signal.
Low energy.

## 3
Clear energy.
Moderate importance.

## 4
Strong emotional charge.
Identity relevance.

## 5
Highly energised, emotionally loaded, central.
Core identity.

# Selection Rules

## Primary Signatures

Top 5 scores.
Always.

---

## Secondary Signatures

Next 3 scores.
Always.

---

# Tie-Break Logic

If tied, use this priority order:

1. Recurring themes  
2. Energy peaks  
3. Pride stories  
4. Frustration evidence

---

# Signature Exclusion Rules

Do not score based on:

- aspirational identity  
- admiration  
- wishful thinking

Only score:

lived evidence.

---

# Emerging Signature Rule

If a signature appears strongly in recent chapters but weak historically:

Tag as:

**Emerging**

Do not promote to Primary unless score qualifies.

Useful for mentor-mode longitudinal tracking.

---

# Final Validation Test

Before finalising:

Ask:

## Does this signature appear across multiple chapters?
If no:
Downgrade.

## Was there energy?
If no:
Downgrade.

## Was there evidence?
If no:
Remove.

---

# Detection Outcome

The final output of the detection engine must produce:

- Top 5 Primary Signatures  
- Top 3 Secondary Signatures  
- Any Emerging Signatures  
- Score table (1–25)  
- Signature evidence map

This detection layer powers the Identity Report.

It is not visible to the user.
It is the intelligence engine beneath the artifact.
