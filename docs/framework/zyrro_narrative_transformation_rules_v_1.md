# Zyrro Narrative Transformation Rules v1

## Core Objective

Transform scored signatures into a report that creates deep recognition.

The user should feel:

**“This is exactly me!”**

Not:

- “That sounds nice.”  
- “That could apply to anyone.”

Recognition must feel:

- specific  
- grounded  
- undeniable  
- emotionally accurate

The goal is not admiration.
The goal is recognition.

---

# Core Writing Principle

Do not write about the signature.

Write about the person through the signature.

### Weak

“You are a Builder.”

### Strong

“You have repeatedly stepped into unstable systems and left behind structure that outlasted your presence.”

Identity must feel lived.
Not labelled.

---

# The Translation Formula

Every signature section must follow:

**Pattern → Evidence → Meaning**

Always.

In this order.

---

## Layer 1 — Pattern

State what the signature fundamentally does.

Not abstractly.
Operationally.

### Weak

“You value truth.”

### Strong

“You orient toward reality faster than comfort.”

Purpose:

Create immediate recognition.

---

## Layer 2 — Evidence

Anchor the pattern in the user’s story.

Mandatory.

Must include:

- actual career events  
- actual transitions  
- actual frustrations  
- actual choices

### Weak

“You often help others.”

### Strong

“You described repeatedly stepping into leadership roles where your real satisfaction came from developing people, not controlling them.”

Evidence makes identity believable.

---

## Layer 3 — Meaning

Explain why it matters.

This is interpretation.
Not advice.

### Weak

“This will help you.”

### Strong

“This explains why environments without growth or movement drain you so quickly.”

Meaning creates emotional clarity.

---

# The Evidence Rule

Every major claim must be backed by evidence.

No exceptions.

Every signature analysis must include:

**Minimum 2 story anchors**

Examples of strong anchors:

- built a company  
- changed careers  
- mentored a team  
- survived a crisis  
- led transformation

Weak anchors:

- likes helping  
- values freedom  
- enjoys creativity

Behaviour is evidence.
Preference is weak evidence.

---

# The Specificity Rule

Use concrete language.

Avoid abstraction.

### Weak

“You enjoy complex challenges.”

### Strong

“You consistently moved toward roles where complexity, uncertainty, and responsibility converged.”

Test:

Can another person picture it?

If no:

Rewrite.

---

# The Repetition Rule

Identity is recurrence.

Language must reflect recurrence.

Use:

- repeatedly  
- consistently  
- across multiple chapters  
- over time  
- again and again

Avoid:

- sometimes  
- occasionally  
- often

The report must feel structural.
Not situational.

---

# The Tension Rule

Strong identity creates friction.

Every primary signature analysis must include:

**One tension**

Examples:

Builder:

“You struggle when momentum depends on people who move slower than you.”

Truth Seeker:

“You become restless in environments built on politeness over honesty.”

Amplifier:

“You overinvest in others and underinvest in yourself.”

Tension increases recognition.

---

# The Frustration Inversion Rule

Frustration reveals identity.

Translate frustrations into values.

Formula:

**What frustrates them = what matters to them**

Example:

“I hate inefficiency.”

Translation:

“You are structured for optimisation.”

Example:

“I hate shallow conversations.”

Translation:

“You are structured for depth.”

---

# The Pride Rule

Pride stories reveal true identity.

When the user lights up:

Use it.

Priority rule:

If a story contains:

- pride  
- energy  
- recurrence

It should strongly shape the narrative.

Pride is high-confidence identity data.

---

# The Language Weight Rule

Different words carry different evidentiary weight.

## High-value verbs

- built  
- led  
- created  
- rebuilt  
- transformed  
- solved  
- taught  
- challenged  
- protected

## Low-value verbs

- liked  
- wanted  
- hoped  
- tried  
- interested

Weight verbs heavily.

Identity is visible in verbs.

---

# The No Generic Praise Rule

Never flatter.

Never inflate.

Avoid:

- exceptional  
- gifted  
- amazing  
- unique

Unless directly evidenced.

The report should feel earned.
Not complimentary.

---

# The No Coaching Rule

Layer 2 is descriptive.
Not directional.

Do not say:

- you should  
- you need  
- your next step  
- now it’s time

That belongs to Layer 3.

---

# The Transferability Rule

Separate profession from identity.

Always.

### Weak

“You are a lawyer.”

### Strong

“You built a career through legal systems, but your deeper pattern is structured truth and complexity navigation.”

Identity must transcend role.

Critical.

---

# Section Writing Rules

## Primary Signatures

Word count:

**150–250 words each**

Must contain:

1. Signature truth  
2. Story evidence  
3. Tension  
4. Why it matters

---

## Secondary Signatures

Word count:

**80–150 words each**

Must contain:

1. Where it appears  
2. Supporting evidence  
3. Latent importance

---

## Constellation Synthesis

Word count:

**200–350 words**

Must explain:

How all five signatures work together.

Not five summaries.

One integrated operating system.

---

# Sentence Pattern Rules

Preferred:

Clear declarative statements.

Example:

“You build structure where ambiguity overwhelms others.”

Avoid hedging.

Avoid:

- may  
- might  
- perhaps  
- could be

Confidence matters.

Only hedge when evidence is weak.

---

# Emotional Accuracy Rule

The goal is not intensity.

The goal is truth.

If the person sounds calm:

Write calmly.

If the person sounds forceful:

Write directly.

Match emotional texture.

Do not over-dramatise.

---

# Recognition Test

Before finalising each section, ask:

## Would this feel specific to this person?

If no:
Rewrite.

---

## Could this apply to 1,000 people?

If yes:
Rewrite.

---

## Is there evidence?

If no:
Rewrite.

---

## Does this create recognition?

If no:
Rewrite.

---

# Final Report Quality Test

Before delivering the full report:

## Specificity Density
Must be high.

## Evidence Density
Must be high.

## Generic Language
Must be low.

## Flattery
Must be low.

## Precision
Must be high.

## Recognition Probability
Must be high.

---

# Final Output Standard

The report is successful only if the user feels:

**“This is exactly me!”**

Not admiration.
Not motivation.
Recognition.
