# Zyrro Discovery Questions

These are the 13 questions asked during the /start questionnaire.
The authoritative source for the app is lib/questions.ts.
This file is the human-readable documentation mirror.
When updating questions, update both files and keep them in sync.

## Q1
**Question:** Give me a quick snapshot of your personal life so far.
**Hint:** Include whatever you think matters. Family, background, where you are in life.

## Q2
**Question:** Tell me about your professional career.
**Hint:** Your current situation, the roles you've held, and how you got here.

## Q3
**Question:** Looking back, what were the most important turning points or shifts in your life or career?
**Hint:** The moments that changed your direction, whether you chose them or not.

## Q4
**Question:** What kind of work or situations have consistently energised you, and what has consistently drained you, regardless of how much you were paid or praised for it?
**Hint:** Across roles, projects, relationships, environments, not just work.

## Q5
**Question:** What kinds of situations or environments keep showing up in your life, even when you try to move away from them?
**Hint:** Recurring frustrations, conflicts, opportunities, or feelings.

## Q6
**Question:** What part of your life or work no longer fits and feels out of alignment?
**Hint:** The gap between how you're spending your time and what actually matters to you.

## Q7
**Question:** What are the signs it is not sustainable?
**Hint:** Your behaviour, mood, motivation, or performance.

## Q8
**Question:** How long has this feeling been building up?
**Hint:** Weeks, months, years. Be honest.

## Q9
**Question:** What tasks or situations feel hardest for you right now?
**Hint:** The things you dread, avoid, or feel drained by.

## Q10
**Question:** What are you avoiding or postponing because of this?
**Hint:** Decisions, conversations, changes. Anything you keep putting off.

## Q11
**Question:** Amid the frustration, what still gives you energy or feels meaningful, even in small bursts?
**Hint:** It doesn't have to be big or obvious.

## Q12
**Question:** When was the last time you felt confident and in your lane? What were you doing?
**Hint:** A role, a project, a moment. However recent or distant.

## Q13
**Question:** If you could change one thing about your current situation, what would it be?
**Hint:** Don't overthink it. What comes to mind first?
