Two issues found in Stage 5's real output: voice varied by persona (Leona/
Katalin second-person, Matteo third-person — the prompt never specified a
voice) and near-identical stock transitions recurred within a single
report ("This pattern is consistent" in 4/8 of Matteo's entries, "the cost
is..." opening nearly every entry across all 3 personas). Added two
positively-framed instructions to the end of `PIPELINE_V2_CALL1_PROMPT`'s
per-signature section (second-person voice requirement; vary-the-phrasing
requirement), per the brief's finding that negative "don't do X" framings
tend to get satisfied narrowly. Re-ran fresh generations for the same 3
personas.

Result: voice fixed cleanly (24/24 entries second-person; the 2 pronoun
hits an automated scan flagged were false positives — "their"/"them"
referring to "projects," not the person, confirmed by manual read).
Phrase repetition improved but not fully solved — the exact Stage 5 tells
are gone or reduced, but a new/residual "You have a [noun phrase]" opening
template recurs 4/8 times in both Leona's and Matteo's sets, and Matteo's
closings lean on a repeated "when you're not engaged in X, you feel
[adjective] and unfulfilled/disengaged" template ("unfulfilled" reused
verbatim 3 times). Logged for Miroslav per the brief's stop condition, not
fixed inline. Regression check (all 8 slots, valid names, no duplicates,
non-empty write-ups) held for all 3 personas; Katalin's grounding
spot-check against her real `discovery_answers` (5 cited specifics)
traced entirely to real evidence.
