### Decisions

- **Attempts stay as soft background:** the options brief must not drop an option because of
  one. The quote checks can't tell whether something really was an attempt; Dana's and Priya's
  kept ones are questionable.
- **One interpretation per conversation:** linking to what the user said earlier, in their
  words, is allowed on any turn. Zyrro's own reading (a theme, a tension, a link to the report)
  is reserved for the one reflection. No design doc change (Miroslav).
- **Removal stays with the Remove control.** A note can add or reword items, never remove
  them. In run D she wrote a removal into a note, so this may need its own decision later.
- **Notes on unchanged risk lines are kept exactly.** No sentence-level overlap check.
- **The Deadline and confirmed-read problems** from direction-and-card went into card-fixes;
  both are now handled in code.
