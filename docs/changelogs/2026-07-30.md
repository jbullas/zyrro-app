## 2026-07-30 — #82 secondary signature compressed format, no forced count

Per `docs/briefs/82-secondary-compressed-format-brief.md`: combines original
C4b (score-0 secondaries written as if evidenced) and C4 (secondaries
generally reading as filler), folded 2026-07-28. Root cause identified
before drafting: Detection Engine never outputs a literal score-0 signature
— the defect came from Layer 2's "exactly 3 entries" requirement forcing
fabrication whenever fewer than 3 real secondary candidates surfaced.

### Changed
- **`lib/prompts/identity-report.ts`**:
  - Removed the forced "exactly 3 entries" requirement for
    `secondary_signature_analysis` — now one entry per item in Detection
    Engine's `secondary_signatures` list (0, 1, 2, or 3), never padded.
  - Added `secondary_signature_summary` — always present, even at zero
    secondaries. Synthesizes the secondary landscape as latent capacities
    relative to the primary constellation when non-empty; states plainly,
    grounded in the actual detection result, that no additional pattern
    surfaced with strong enough evidence when empty — no apologetic/hedging
    language.
  - Replaced the old 80–150 word independent paragraph format with a
    compressed, comparative format: no word floor, ~100–120 word ceiling,
    must anchor to one specific evidence circumstance, must contrast against
    exactly one named primary using the fixed phrase "your primary [Name]
    signature," must not reuse the primary's own `evidence_analysis` text,
    plain text only (no bold/markdown — that's #90, separate).
  - Added source-discipline and evidence-depth-tier instructions (only cite
    evidence_units tagged to this exact secondary; 0–1 tagged units → short
    fallback instead of a full narrative) — see Decisions below on why this
    is only partially reliable.
  - Updated the `## OUTPUT FORMAT` verification block and `## STRUCTURAL
    CONSTRAINTS` accordingly. Bumped `schema_version` to `"1.1"`.
- **`lib/artifact-schemas.ts`**: `secondary_signature_analysis` typed as
  0–3 entries; added `secondary_signature_summary: string`; `schema_version`
  widened to `"1.0" | "1.1"`. `signature_profile_summary.secondary_signatures`
  deliberately left at "exactly 3" — that's #83's scope, not folded in here
  (flagged, not silently coupled — see Decisions).
- **`app/identity/page.tsx`**: renders 0/1/2/3 secondary cases without empty
  card slots or broken grid; Section 3's mini bars card is hidden entirely
  at zero secondaries; reworded `SCORING_EXPLANATION` copy that previously
  asserted a fixed "the next three are your Secondary Signatures" — no
  longer accurate once count is variable.
- **`lib/generate-identity-report.ts`**: added `enforceSecondaryEvidenceFloor`
  — a deterministic, code-level backstop. After Layer 2 returns, any
  `secondary_signature_analysis` entry with zero `evidence_units` tagged
  `secondary_signature_candidate` to its exact name has its `analysis` text
  discarded and replaced with a fixed, non-LLM fallback sentence. This is
  the actual fix for the core defect (C4b) — see Decisions.

### Decisions
- **`signature_profile_summary.secondary_signatures` left at "exactly 3"
  on purpose.** It carries the identical fabrication risk this ticket
  fixes for `secondary_signature_analysis` (unrendered on `/identity`
  today, but internally inconsistent once counts diverge). Flagged
  explicitly rather than folded in silently — that's #83's territory to
  decide, not assumed here.
- **Three prompt-wording rounds did not close the fabrication/misattribution
  gap — same "inconsistent model compliance" pattern as C5 (2026-07-29).**
  Iteration history:
  1. First edit: distinguish "the evidence clause" from "the situation
     behind it," mirroring `cover.identity_thesis`'s distillation mechanic,
     plus a labeled Weak/forbidden example. Tested against a fixed
     synthetic rich-answer case (Resonator/Activator/Finisher) — the model
     reproduced the forbidden example almost verbatim instead of avoiding
     it, and Finisher fabricated an ungrounded detail ("especially when
     completing tasks") not present in its own source evidence.
  2. Real-user check (read-only, no writes to real accounts) against 3 of
     the 12 real users (Keiko, Soren, Nadia) surfaced a more precise and
     more concerning failure than the synthetic case: **misattribution**,
     not just clause-lifting. Soren/Optimizer had zero evidence_units
     tagged to it at all yet got a confident narrative; Nadia/Finisher had
     3 tagged units of its own but cross-borrowed a phrase tagged to
     Architect instead. 2 of 6 real populated entries affected (33%).
  3. Second edit: added explicit source-discipline (cite only evidence_units
     where `secondary_signature_candidate` matches this exact signature)
     plus evidence-depth tiers (0–1 tagged units → short fallback, no forced
     narrative or primary contrast; 2+ → full narrative). Re-tested against
     the same 3 real users: the tier rule was not reliably followed — 2 of
     3 populated entries in that round ignored it outright, including one
     (Nadia/Futurist, 0 tagged units) that fabricated an even richer
     ungrounded narrative ("reflections on time and the risks of waiting
     for 'someday'") than anything seen previously.
  - Per session decision, capped iteration at this point rather than
    continuing to patch wording.
- **Split the two remaining failure modes rather than treating them as one
  gap:**
  1. **Zero-tagged-evidence entries (fabrication) — fixed in code, in scope
     for #82.** `enforceSecondaryEvidenceFloor` deterministically overrides
     any entry with zero matching evidence_units, regardless of what Layer 2
     generated. Verified: caught 2/2 zero-evidence entries in a follow-up
     real-user run (Keiko/Pioneer, Keiko/Synthesizer), left all 5/5 entries
     with real tagged evidence untouched in the same run.
  2. **≤1-tagged-unit entries that stay evidence-accurate but ignore the
     short-form tier (e.g. Soren's Catalyst-style cases) — accepted as a
     known, logged gap, not pursued further. Miroslav's explicit sign-off,
     verbatim:**
     > Stopping prompt iteration here — this is the C5 "inconsistent
     > compliance" pattern, not a wording problem... Catalyst-style misses
     > (grounded in its own tagged evidence, but skipped the ≤1-unit
     > short-form tier and wrote a full narrative anyway): this is a
     > formatting-discipline gap, not an evidence violation. Log it in the
     > ticket as a known, accepted gap per Definition of Done path 3 — I'm
     > giving explicit sign-off on this specific risk: the model doesn't
     > always respect the tier-length rule even with sufficient/grounded
     > evidence, but content stays evidence-accurate. Not worth a 4th
     > prompt round.
