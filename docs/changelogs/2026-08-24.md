### Decisions

- Reused `PrimarySignatureBars` as-is (structurally) for secondary data
  per the brief's explicit instruction, accepting that the combined
  chart shows secondary bars in the same unmuted visual treatment as
  primary (not the muted look used elsewhere on the page) — the brief
  pre-resolved this as expected behavior, not left ambiguous.
- The badge-restart issue was not something the brief's text flagged —
  it surfaced only once the combined chart was actually rendered and
  inspected. Fixed immediately rather than shipped with a known
  inconsistency, since it directly contradicts numbering the Secondary
  Signatures section already uses for the same data on the same page.
- Added `startBadge` as an optional prop with a safe default rather than
  a new component or a one-off inline renumbering, to keep the change
  small and contained per the brief's "single file unless the prop needs
  to live in the component" framing.
